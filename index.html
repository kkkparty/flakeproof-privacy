Flakeproof 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 28 日 ｜ 开发者：刘 中鑫（Zhongxin Liu）｜ 联系邮箱：840137950@qq.com

Flakeproof（以下简称「本应用」）是一款本地优先的工具应用。我们尽量少地处理您的数据：本应用无账号系统、无广告、无第三方统计与追踪 SDK，功能内容默认只保存在您的设备本机。

一、我们收集什么

本应用不收集、不上传您的个人数据。您创建的内容（笔记、卡片草稿、活动信息、搜索偏好等）均保存在设备本地（应用沙盒 / 系统偏好设置），随应用卸载一并删除。

二、设备权限

  • 相册（仅添加）：仅当您主动点击「存相册」时，把您生成的图片写入相册。本应用不会读取您的相册内容。
  • 定位（如授权）：仅在您使用距离相关功能时申请，且只在本机用于距离显示，不上传、不存储、不做后台追踪。

三、第三方服务

  • 高德地图 Web 服务：当您使用「实分」的地点搜索时，您输入的搜索关键词与城市名会发送至高德开放平台（restapi.amap.com）以返回结果。该请求不包含您的账号或身份信息。高德对请求的处理受其自身隐私政策约束：https://lbs.amap.com/page/privacy。
  • 「红图」渲染在本机完成；但若您粘贴的 HTML 自身引用了外部图片 / 字体等资源，加载这些资源会产生相应的网络访问，取决于您粘贴内容本身。

四、数据存储与删除

  • 所有内容仅存本机，我们没有服务器保存您的数据。
  • 删除应用即删除全部本地数据；「重写 / 清空」操作可随时移除单条内容。
  • 分享导出的临时文件在使用后由系统回收。

五、儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童信息。

六、政策变更

如本政策有任何更新，我们会发布在本页面并更新生效日期。重大变更会在应用内适当位置提示。

七、联系我们

Privacy Policy

Effective: October 28, 2026 · Developer: Zhongxin Liu · 840137950@qq.com

Flakeproof (the "App") is a local-first utility app. We handle as little of your data as possible: the App has no account system, no ads, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Your content stays on your device by default.

1. What We Collect

We do not collect or upload your personal data. Content you create (notes, card drafts, activities, preferences) is stored locally on your device and removed when you delete the App.

2. Device Permissions

  • Photo Library (add only): only when you tap "Save", generated images are written to your photo library. We never read your library.
  • Location (if authorized): requested only while you use distance-related features, used on-device for distance display only. Never uploaded, never stored, never tracked in the background.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Amap Web Service: when you use place search in "HonestPin", your search keywords and city name are sent to Amap (restapi.amap.com) to return results. No account or identity information is included. Amap's handling is governed by its own privacy policy: https://lbs.amap.com/page/privacy.
  • "Red Card" rendering happens on-device; however, if pasted HTML references external images/fonts, loading them generates network access determined by your pasted content.

4. Storage & Deletion

  • All content is stored on-device only; we have no server holding your data.
  • Deleting the App removes all local data; "Reset" removes individual items at any time.
  • Temporary export files are reclaimed by the system after use.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Changes

Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes will be surfaced in the App where appropriate.
